# Step 6: Final company submission

## Submission workflow

1. After company assignments are published, the administrator opens the final
   submission stage.
2. Each assigned team sees its company and the final submission requirements.
3. The team leader saves a draft and may edit it until submission.
4. The backend validates the required fields when the leader submits.
5. A successful submission is locked and made available to the administrator
   and any assigned evaluators.
6. An administrator may reopen an accidental submission through an explicit
   action with a recorded reason.

## Required fields

- Title (required)
- Description (required)
- Source or reference link (required)

The backend must enforce the required fields and validate the source link.

## Completion conditions

- Every assigned team has submitted the required information, or has an
  administrator-approved exception.
- All final submissions are locked and auditable.
